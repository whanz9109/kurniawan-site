Wave energy converters — floating absorber hydrodynamics & wave resource

Potential flow (CPM, HOBEM, BEM), Ansys Aqwa, multi-objective optimisation · 10 projects
  1. 2023
    Desain Kinematika Silinder Wave Energy Converter dan Analisa Hidrodinamika Floating Absorber Menggunakan Metode Boundary Element Method
    Muhamad Ainun Nazzrin Chuzain

    RAOs and hydrodynamic forces from Ansys Aqwa compared with CPM and HOBEM; the optimal diameter-to-draft ratio is about 0.67–1.0.

  2. 2023
    Analisis Hidrodinamika Floating Absorber dalam Kondisi Gelombang Linear dengan Constant Panel Method dan Higher Order Boundary Element Method
    Gema Putra Rahardjo

    Diameter-to-draft ratios evaluated through RAOs and hydrodynamic forces; an optimal ratio of 1.0 for the wave conditions studied.

  3. 2023
    Analisis Hidrodinamika Floating Absorber dalam Keadaan Gelombang Non-Linear dengan Boundary Element Method
    Nathanael Roberts Brilian

    Motion RAOs and wave exciting forces for varying floater drafts, for the north coast of the Java Sea.

  4. 2023
    Hindcasting Potensi Gelombang di Indonesia Menggunakan Data NOAA dan ERA5 untuk Wave Energy Converter
    Renaldi Haryo Yudho

    Maps Indonesian wave energy potential from NOAA and ERA5 hindcast data, with a WEC cost model (LCOE).

  5. 2024
    Kinematic Design of Multi-Cylinder Wave Energy Converter and Floating Absorber Hydrodynamic Analysis Using Ansys Aqwa
    Erwin

    Kinematic design of a multi-cylinder WEC with varied floater shapes to maximise power output.

  6. 2024
    Hydrodynamic Performance and Multi-Objective Optimization of Multi-Cylinder Floating Point Absorber Wave Energy Converter
    Wildan Amarullah ArrosyidPublished

    Floater geometry optimised for Pelabuhanratu with RSM, ANN, MOGA and MCDM, balancing capture width ratio against cost; outer radius matters most.

    Read the article (Ocean Engineering, 2025) ↗
  7. 2025
    Optimasi Konfigurasi WEC Array dengan Algoritma Evolusi dan Central Composite Design di Laut Selatan Jawa
    Daffa Sulaiman KamilIn progress

    Optimises WEC array layouts for Java’s southern coast using evolutionary algorithms and central composite design.

  8. 2025
    Hindcasting Potensi Gelombang di Indonesia Menggunakan Data NOAA dan ERA5 dan Analisa Kelayakan Ekonomi untuk Wave Energy Converter
    Rionaldi Hamonangan Hutagalung

    Extends the wave resource mapping with a WEC economic feasibility study, including payback period and net present value.

  9. 2025
    Analisis Hidrodinamika, Kinematika, dan Dinamika Multi Cylinder Wave Energy Converter: Studi Kasus Data Gelombang Pelabuhanratu, Jawa Barat
    Wildan Amarullah ArrosyidDoctoral dissertation

    Doctoral dissertation drawing together the multi-cylinder WEC work for Pelabuhanratu. Spin-offs include a review of WEC optimisation in Ocean Engineering (2025) and a floater-geometry paper under revision.

    Read the review article (Ocean Engineering, 2025) ↗
  10. 2026
    Rancang Bangun dan Validasi Smartbuoy Berbasis Sensor Inersia untuk Pemantauan Karakteristik Gelombang Laut
    Kevin Pradivta Wijaya Putra

    A low-cost inertial-sensor smartbuoy as an alternative to imported wave buoys, characterised with pendulum, inclining and free-decay tests.

Wave energy converters — hydraulic power take-off (HPTO)

MATLAB/Simscape, SQP, genetic algorithms, hydraulic accumulators · 8 projects
  1. 2023
    Optimasi Desain Parameter Hydraulic Power Take-Off untuk Wave Energy Converter dengan Variasi Ukuran Selang dan Katup dengan Metode Sequential Quadratic Programming
    Juan Anthonius Cladio Siahaan

    Optimises HPTO hose and inlet-valve sizes under constraints taken from catalogue component specifications.

  2. 2023
    Estimasi Parameter Hydraulic Power Take-Off pada Wave Energy Converter Tipe Floating Absorber dengan Metode Genetic Algorithm
    Vincent Brendli Kusuma

    Estimates HPTO component parameters with a genetic algorithm and compares performance across different numbers of absorbers.

  3. 2023
    Analisis Power Smoothing dan Performa Rancangan Hydraulic Power Take-Off dengan Menggunakan Akumulator pada Wave Energy Converter
    Teuku Muzaki

    How gas/bladder accumulator volume and precharge pressure affect HPTO torque, motor speed and power smoothing.

  4. 2023
    Hydraulic Power Take-Off Systems Unit dengan Optimasi Parameter Multi-Point Floating Absorbers Wave Energy Converter
    Ario GeraldiResearch proposal

    Wave forces from multi-point absorber hydrodynamics feed an HPTO simulation in MATLAB/Simscape to size the unit.

  5. 2024
    Analisis Perbandingan Performa Akumulator Tipe Gas-Charged dan Spring-Loaded dalam Menyimpan Energi pada Rancangan Sistem Hydraulic Power Take-Off di dalam Wave Energy Converter
    Andang Deva Ardhiansyah

    Spring-loaded accumulators stored energy better than the commonly used gas-charged type.

  6. 2024
    Optimasi Desain Sistem Hydraulic Power Take-Off (HPTO) pada Wave Energy Converter Bertipe Single Point Floating Absorber dengan Silinder Ganda dan Silinder Tiga
    Dwi Febrianto Wibowo

    Compares two- and three-cylinder HPTO layouts in MATLAB Simscape, optimised with Response Optimizer (SQP).

  7. 2025
    Studi Kinerja Sistem Hydraulic Power Take-Off (HPTO) pada Wave Energy Converter dengan Variasi Parameter Gerakan Gelombang Teratur Menggunakan Penggerak Aktuator Pneumatik
    Farhan Abdurrahman

    A pneumatic actuator reproduces regular waves in the lab; HPTO pressure losses and motor efficiency are measured for three wave periods.

  8. 2026
    Studi Eksperimental Pengaruh Bukaan Flow Control Valve terhadap Karakteristik Damping Hydraulic dan Kinerja Sistem Wave Energy Converter Hydraulic Power Take-Off
    Asjad Rasyiq Habibi

    Experiments on how flow control valve opening sets hydraulic damping and HPTO performance.
